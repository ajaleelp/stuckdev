The StuckDev Code of Conduct
  • 1.  Ensure you have researched enough by yourself online before reaching out to folks. Do not bother them with issues that are just a Google search away!
  • 2.  Don't be a nagger! Being stuck can be frustrating. But stay away from repeatedly reaching out with the same issue. Respect people's time.
  • 3.  Be careful with what you share on-screen. Do not breach any contracts by sharing private code. If you are helping out someone, ensure they are not sharing any confidential data.
Happy debugging folks!
